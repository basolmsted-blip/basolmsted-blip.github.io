The research

I supported long-term research into primate behavioral ecology, social structure, and collective action. My fieldwork focused on adult female participation in simulated intergroup engagements across three habituated groups of red-tailed monkeys (Cercopithecus ascanius).

Field practice

  • Dawn-to-dusk focal follows, scan samples, and continuous behavioral observations.
  • Playback experiments simulating territorial incursions and measuring collective defense.
  • Non-invasive fecal and urine sampling for hormone and genetic analysis.
  • Field-log transcription and group demographic and ranging records.

What it changed

The field season made the demands of long-term behavioral research tangible: patient observation, careful identification, reliable records, and close collaboration with expert local trackers. It deepened my interest in how sociality and space can be studied across disciplines.
